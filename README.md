# RANOGO

### Digital infrastructure for access to essential services in Africa

## The problem

The people and organizations involved in a patient's care sometimes work with separate records and systems. The patient then has to connect the dots between the clinician, laboratory, pharmacy, and insurer. When parts of the record are on paper, patients may have to carry their reports while clinicians search for documents kept at their own facilities. A missing result or delayed information can interrupt follow-up.

## Our objective

RANOGO aims to connect patients, care teams, laboratories, pharmacies, and insurers so each authorized participant can provide or see the information relevant to their role. This requires interoperability, meaning existing systems can exchange useful information among authorized participants. A laboratory could make a result available to an authorized clinician, while an insurer could receive the documents it needs to verify coverage. For patients, the goal is simpler follow-up, including when they change clinicians or facilities.

## Our vision

Prenatal care is the starting point. Over time, RANOGO aims to support continuity of care at every age and help people access services such as social protection and education where relevant programs are available. **Benin and Burkina Faso** are the first planned pilot countries.

## What to get right

Protect patient data and access rights, account for differences in internet and device access, and work with existing local partners.

## The demonstration MVP

The MVP focuses on maternal and child health. Its first demonstration scenario is antenatal care, using synthetic data to show patient registration with consent, antenatal visit recording, and district-level supervision.

## Roadmap

These milestones outline planned capabilities.

1. **Maternal and child health MVP.** The first demonstrated workflow covers registration with consent and prenatal follow-up. The demonstration with synthetic data is targeted for December 2026 and a limited pilot for January 2027, subject to readiness and required approvals.
2. **Longitudinal health record.** Give people a health history at every age, with relevant visits and results available across providers to authorized care teams.
3. **Health coverage and social benefits.** Help patients verify and track health coverage and eligible families access and track public benefits. This includes tracking related reimbursements or payments under the applicable contracts or program rules.
4. **Education and other essential services.** Extend access to selected education-related and other essential services as local needs and programs allow.

## User journeys

### Health worker

1. Sign in to a facility-scoped workspace.
2. Register a patient after recording informed consent.
3. Record antenatal visits and review care history.
4. Use dashboard indicators and notifications to prioritize follow-up.

### District supervisor

1. Open a district-level operational overview.
2. Review patient, visit, and health-worker activity.
3. Investigate high-risk or overdue cases.
4. Export a bounded report for an authorized reporting period.
